Dispatch sounds preview

The Dispatch app's own notification sounds next to the shared modern sounds the other apps keep. Open this file from the Omni checkout (it plays the WAV files by relative path). Levels are the loudest 400 ms (EBU R128 momentary, as played on two speakers) and the true peak.

  • Timbre: a soft FM "console mallet" (vibraphone-like bar, bright strike that mellows) with a slow motor tremolo on ringing notes; alarms use a brighter 2:1 FM voice and faster tremolo.
  • Signature: every sound opens with the console tap, a short grace note a perfect fifth below the note it leads into.
  • Key: every note from D major pentatonic. Each sound keeps the loudness and length class of the shared sound it replaces.
  • Regenerate: node scripts/notification-sounds/generate-dispatch-sounds.mjs (see scripts/notification-sounds/README.md).
